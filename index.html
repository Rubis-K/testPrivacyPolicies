Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Synapse AI Manager ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you connect and use our AI Instagram Account Manager platform (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

To provide automated messaging, AI assistance, media sharing, and order processing services, we process the following categories of data via Meta's APIs:

  • Instagram Profile & Account Information: Scoped user IDs, account handles, and access tokens required to authenticate and connect your Instagram Business or Creator account.
  • Messaging Content: Direct messages (DMs), incoming customer inquiries, message timestamps, and conversation context exchanged between customers and the account.
  • Media & Attachments: Images, videos, stickers, or attachments sent or received within the conversation window to facilitate automated replies and product showcases.
  • Order & Transaction Details: Customer order information, including items requested, sizes, delivery preferences, or contact details shared within messages to process transactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Generating automated AI responses to customer inquiries on your behalf.
  • Sending requested product images, media, or promotional materials via Instagram Messaging.
  • Parsing conversation details to track order states, product inquiries, and customer management workflows.
  • Maintaining chat history context to deliver relevant and continuous automated conversations.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties. Data is processed solely through secure infrastructure and essential service providers:

  • Meta Graph API: Used to send and receive Instagram direct messages in compliance with Meta Developer Policies.
  • AI Processing Providers: Message text may be processed using artificial intelligence models strictly to generate contextual responses.
  • Database & Cloud Hosting: Secure database services used exclusively to store conversation state and order logs.

4. Data Retention & Security

We store conversation history and order logs only as long as necessary to maintain contextual assistance and process orders. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL) and secure database access controls to safeguard all data.

5. Data Deletion Instructions

You and your users have full control over stored data:

  • Account Disconnection: You can revoke access at any time by removing our application from your Instagram account settings under Settings & Privacy > Apps and Websites.
  • Data Erasure Requests: To request complete deletion of your account data, chat logs, or order records from our databases, contact us at ruba1004pr@gmail.com. All associated data will be permanently removed within 30 days.
